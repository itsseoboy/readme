# FreeKrillion

FreeKrillion is a free trivia game where the goal is to give the rarest correct answer, not just the most obvious one. Each dive features seven timed prompts, and rarer answers earn more points. Play at [freekrillion.com](https://freekrillion.com).

## Features

- **Daily Dive:** Answer the same seven prompts as everyone else each day.
- **Unlimited mode:** Keep diving with new sets of prompts.
- **Practice mode:** Warm up and learn the game without pressure.
- **Multiplayer:** Compete in pass-and-play games or online rooms.
- **Rarity-based scoring:** Earn more points for answers fewer players give, with your score reflected as an ocean depth.
- **Free to play:** No ads or premium tier. Play as a guest; sign in with Google for permanent stats and online multiplayer.
